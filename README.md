Welcome to my personal research and technical portfolio.

I am Praise-EL Michaels, Ph.D., an AI/ML researcher and engineer with a research focus on Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, computational mental health, machine learning, and responsible AI.

My work explores how artificial intelligence can be used to understand human communication and behavior, particularly how people express distress, seek social support, discuss anxiety and loneliness, and communicate across linguistic and demographic contexts.

This portfolio brings together selected publications, research projects, machine learning applications, technical skills, and deployed systems.
